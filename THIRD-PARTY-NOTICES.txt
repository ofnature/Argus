Argus bundles or derives from the following third-party work.

SubmarineTracker — https://github.com/Infiziert90/SubmarineTracker
  Copyright (c) 2023 Infi <infiziert@protonmail.ch>. MIT License.
  Argus ports the submarine voyage model from this project: per-sector
  surveillance/retrieval/favor breakpoints, EXP bonus tiers, rank/part stat
  composition, voyage distance and duration formulas, and the sector unlock
  chain (Argus/Core/Data/SubmarineData.cs and Argus/Core/Calc/*). The sector
  breakpoint data originates from the community spreadsheet credited in that
  project (docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eiuGRrQTjJ5n4ogybKOBxJRT7EiJLlX93fge-Z8MmD4).

ECommons — https://github.com/NightmareXIV/ECommons
  Copyright (c) 2023 NightmareXIV. MIT License. Included as a git submodule.

AutoRetainer — https://github.com/PunishXIV/AutoRetainer
  The technique for selecting a destination in the in-game voyage planner
  (dispatching AtkEventType 35 to the AirShipExploration addon with an index
  payload) follows AutoRetainer's implementation.

FFXIV Airship Log Data — community spreadsheet maintained by Nyalia / Neri Feralheart
  (docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1lv43Af1vOnco4narozL6JebwO5KuQA6uckMuYG8VyFE)
  and submarine.girin.dev — used to seed airship sector surveillance tiers and
  the airship EXP rating estimate (Argus/Core/Data/AirshipData.cs).

FINAL FANTASY XIV © SQUARE ENIX CO., LTD. Game data (sector, part and rank
tables) is read from the game client at runtime via Lumina.
